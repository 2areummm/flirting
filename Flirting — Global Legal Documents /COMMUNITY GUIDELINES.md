COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Welcome to Flirting! These guidelines exist to keep our community safe, respectful, and enjoyable for everyone. By using Flirting, you agree to follow these rules.

1. Be Authentic
✅ DO:

Use real photos of yourself
Provide accurate information
Be honest about your age, relationship status, and intentions
❌ DON'T:

Use photos of other people, celebrities, or AI-generated images
Impersonate others
Create fake profiles
Lie about your identity or age
2. Be Respectful
✅ DO:

Treat others as you'd want to be treated
Respect when someone says no
Accept rejection gracefully
❌ DON'T:

Harass, threaten, or intimidate other users
Send unwanted explicit messages or images
Use hate speech, slurs, or discriminatory language
Body shame, slut shame, or insult others
Stalk users on or off the platform
3. Keep It Safe
✅ DO:

Meet in public places
Tell a friend about your dates
Trust your instincts
Report suspicious behavior
❌ DON'T:

Share financial information
Send money to people you haven't met
Share home addresses on your profile
Meet anyone who pressures you for private locations
4. No Nudity or Sexual Content
❌ PROHIBITED:

Nude or partially nude photos
Sexually suggestive imagery
Lingerie, swimwear in suggestive poses (regular beach photos are OK)
Explicit sexual descriptions in bios
Soliciting sexual services
Sharing intimate content of others
Violations result in immediate removal and may lead to permanent bans.

5. No Illegal Activity
❌ PROHIBITED:

Promoting or selling drugs
Soliciting prostitution
Promoting violence or terrorism
Sharing weapons or illegal goods
Human trafficking (zero tolerance)
Underage content (zero tolerance — reported to authorities)
6. No Scams or Spam
❌ PROHIBITED:

Romance scams
Cryptocurrency or investment pitches
Promoting other apps, websites, or services
Multi-level marketing recruitment
Repeatedly sending the same message
Phishing attempts
7. Protect Privacy
✅ DO:

Respect others' boundaries
Ask before sharing photos
❌ DON'T:

Share screenshots of private conversations publicly
Doxx other users (revealing personal info)
Share others' photos without permission
Stalk or surveil other users
8. Cultural Respect
We have users from diverse backgrounds. Please:

Respect religious and cultural differences
Avoid mocking religions, beliefs, or traditions
Be mindful of cultural sensitivities in different regions
9. Report Misconduct
If you see violations of these guidelines:

Use the Report button on profiles or messages
Block users who make you uncomfortable
Contact info@evmodu.kr for urgent matters
Contact local authorities for criminal matters
10. Consequences
Violations may result in:

Warning: First-time minor violations
Content Removal: Inappropriate photos or messages
Temporary Suspension: 1–30 days
Permanent Ban: Serious or repeated violations
Severe violations (sexual content involving minors, threats of violence, illegal activity) result in immediate permanent ban and may be reported to law enforcement.

11. Appeals
If you believe you were sanctioned in error, email info@evmodu.kr within 14 days with:

Your account ID
The action taken
Why you believe it was incorrect
We review appeals within 7 business days.

12. Together, We Make Flirting Better
Our community thrives when everyone contributes to a safe, respectful space. Thank you for being part of Flirting.
